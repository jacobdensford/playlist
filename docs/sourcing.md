### DVD or Blu-Ray

Get blu-ray, unless:

- Shot on tape (TV before around 2007) or digital before around 2010
- Blu-ray has a bad reputation (soft, artificially sharpened, "waxy" look, etc)
- Flat, simple 2D animation

### Widescreen or Fullscreen

Get widescreen, unless:

- Movie from before around 1953
- TV show from before around 2007

### DVDs

- Watch for gold-ish colored disks or "dual layer"—DVD-9—for generally  better quality
- Watch for anamorphic widescreen or "enhanced for 16:9 widescreen TVs"
