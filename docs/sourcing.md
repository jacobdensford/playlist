### DVDs

- Watch for  gold-ish colored disks—DVD-9—for better quality
- Also try to get widescreen, specifically anamorphic widescreen or "enhanced for 16:9 widescreen TVs", except for movies made before 1953 and TV shows made before 2007 or so
